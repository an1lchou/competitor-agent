## Samsung
Website: https://www.samsung.com
Reason: Samsung is Apple’s biggest hardware competitor, directly competing in smartphones, tablets, wearables, and earbuds, with global phone sales closely matching Apple’s.

### Pricing
- Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold8 is priced at $1,899. ([source](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bharathsrinivas25_apple-samsung-brandstrategy-activity-7503816336733573120-_zbj)) (weak source)
- Samsung sells phones starting from around 8,000 rupees in India. ([source](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bharathsrinivas25_apple-samsung-brandstrategy-activity-7503816336733573120-_zbj)) (weak source)

### Features
- Samsung's Galaxy devices support on-device AI processing via NPU chips. ([source](https://www.studocu.vn/vn/document/dai-hoc-kinh-te-quoc-dan/marketing/position-fhhhh/146824031)) (weak source)
- Samsung offers features like Circle to Search and wider language support in its AI capabilities. ([source](https://www.studocu.vn/vn/document/dai-hoc-kinh-te-quoc-dan/marketing/position-fhhhh/146824031)) (weak source)
- Samsung Galaxy devices offer customization, fast charging, zoom, and Galaxy AI features. ([source](https://miamicenters.com/iphone-vs-samsung-2026)) (weak source)

### Positioning
- Samsung positions itself as innovative and accessible with wide market appeal. ([source](https://ijsrem.com/download/a-comparative-study-of-brand-positioning-strategies-apple-vs-samsung/fulltext.pdf))

### News
- Samsung announced the launch of the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8, Z Flip8, Watch Ultra2, and Watch9 on July 22, 2026. ([source](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/galaxy-unpacked))
- Samsung launched the One UI 9.5 beta program on November 18, 2026, ahead of its official release. ([source](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-begins-one-ui-9-5-beta-program))

## Google
Website: https://www.google.com
Reason: Google competes directly with Apple through its Android operating system, Pixel devices, and ecosystem services like the Google Play Store and cloud platforms.

### Pricing
- Google AI Ultra plan costs $199.99/month and includes 30 TB of cloud storage. ([source](https://subbuddy.io/en/services/compare/google-one-vs-apple-one)) (weak source)

### Features
- Google Maps uses augmented reality with visual positioning system (VPS) for turn-by-turn navigation via camera. ([source](https://www.inc.com/associated-press/google-new-products-io-developer-conference-maps-duplex-gmail.html))

### Positioning
- Google positions Android and its ecosystem as seamlessly integrated with AI features for daily assistance. ([source](https://about.google/products))
- Google emphasizes AI agent capabilities that perform tasks autonomously, moving beyond simple assistant functions. ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gwXQzcA3pMY)) (weak source)
- Google positions its ecosystem as more affordable and open compared to Apple’s tightly integrated but restrictive environment. ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1yC5KTY_8Ag)) (weak source)

### News
- Google launched Tool Retrieval in the Gemini API on November 19, 2026, allowing on-demand loading of agent tool schemas. ([source](https://iacompa.com/news/companies/google)) (weak source)
- Google demonstrated autonomous AI agents at I/O 2026 capable of performing background tasks across apps and services. ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gwXQzcA3pMY)) (weak source)

## Microsoft
Website: https://www.microsoft.com
Reason: Microsoft is a major competitor in personal computing with its Windows operating system and Surface devices, challenging Apple in operating systems and integrated hardware-software ecosystems.

### Pricing
- Office 2024 is available as a one-time purchase with a single up-front cost for PCs and Macs, with no free upgrade to future major releases. ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/buy/compare-all-microsoft-365-products))
- Power BI offers a free account for creating interactive reports, and also provides Pro and Premium subscription plans for advanced features. ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-fabric/features/power-bi))

### Features
- Microsoft 365 Education A7 includes advanced AI, security, governance, and device management capabilities for educational institutions. ([source](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-november))
- Copilot in Microsoft 365 allows secure use with work files and offers higher AI usage limits compared to the free version. ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/buy/compare-all-microsoft-365-products))

### Positioning
- Microsoft positions itself as a provider of flexible software solutions across a wide range of hardware, contrasting with Apple’s integrated ecosystem. ([source](https://businessmodelanalyst.com?p=997957)) (weak source)
- Microsoft uses multi-segment positioning to target diverse consumer groups including students, professionals, and enterprises. ([source](https://www.pdfagile.com/blog/microsoft-segmentation-targeting-and-positioning)) (weak source)
- Microsoft is positioned as a Leader in Gartner’s 2026 Magic Quadrant for Analytics and Business Intelligence Platforms. ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-fabric/features/power-bi))

### News
- At Microsoft Ignite 2026, the company expanded the public preview of Integrated Security Operations Center (ISOC) in Microsoft Defender. ([source](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-november))
