# Competitor briefing: Google

Date: 2026-11-24

## Microsoft
Website: https://www.microsoft.com
Reason: Microsoft competes directly with Google in search (via Bing), cloud computing (via Azure), and AI (via Copilot), making it a top multi-front rival.

### Pricing
- Microsoft 365 Business Basic costs $7.00 per user per month when paid yearly. ([source](https://www.netsysgroup.com/blog/microsoft-365-vs-google-workspace-small-business)) (weak source)
- Microsoft 365 Business Premium with Copilot is priced at $32.00 per user per month when paid yearly. ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-plans-and-pricing))

### Features
- Microsoft 365 includes desktop apps like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint with AI-powered Microsoft Copilot across its business and consumer plans. ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/buy/compare-all-microsoft-365-products))
- Microsoft 365 offers 1TB of OneDrive cloud storage per user in its Business Basic plan, compared to Google Workspace's 30GB at the same price tier. ([source](https://www.suitebriar.com/blog/google-workspace-vs-microsoft-365-market-share)) (weak source)
- Microsoft bundles device management and endpoint detection via Intune into its Microsoft 365 Business Premium plan, providing deeper IT administration capabilities. ([source](https://www.netsysgroup.com/blog/microsoft-365-vs-google-workspace-small-business)) (weak source)

### Positioning
- Microsoft positions itself as a leader in enterprise solutions with deep IT administration, security, and compliance features tailored for growing businesses. ([source](https://www.ciotech.us/microsoft-365-vs-google-workspace-better-decision-2026)) (weak source)
- Microsoft is positioned as a Leader in the Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Analytics and Business Intelligence Platforms 2026 due to its Power BI and Fabric offerings. ([source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-fabric/features/power-bi))

## Amazon
Website: https://www.amazon.com
Reason: Amazon rivals Google in cloud services through AWS and in digital advertising, while also competing in product search and AI infrastructure.

### Pricing
- Amazon uses dynamic pricing, adjusting prices in real-time based on factors like supply and demand, competition, and customer behavior. ([source](https://www.boardfy.com/a-guide-to-amazons-pricing-strategy)) (weak source)
- Amazon adjusts prices on tens of millions of SKUs multiple times daily, with a repricing cadence as fast as every ten minutes in competitive categories. ([source](https://e-cens.com/amazons-pricing-strategy-why-copying-it-fails-and-what-to-do-instead)) (weak source)
- Amazon consistently offers the lowest online prices among major U.S. retailers, with prices averaging 14% lower than competitors in 2025. ([source](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-pricing))

### Features
- Amazon uses purchase history to optimize product recommendations and seller rankings, enhancing personalization and relevance. ([source](https://fourweekmba.com/google-vs-amazon)) (weak source)
- Amazon offers Prime delivery with 48-hour standard shipping through its global network of 610 fulfillment centers. ([source](https://fourweekmba.com/google-vs-amazon)) (weak source)
- Amazon allows merchants to use the 'Buy With Prime' button on their websites, enabling Prime benefits like payment and fulfillment services. ([source](https://www.businessinsider.com/how-google-is-competing-with-amazon-for-shoppers-2022-5)) (weak source)

### Positioning
- Amazon positions itself as a customer-obsessed retailer offering convenience, endless selection, and value, akin to an e-commerce 'wish-granting genie'. ([source](https://netpeak.us/blog/amazon-brand-positioning-strategies-and-key-insights-to-dominate-niche-audiences)) (weak source)
- Amazon’s brand positioning emphasizes low prices, wide selection, and convenience, reinforcing its image as a universal product provider. ([source](https://fabrikbrands.com/insights/brand-analysis/amazon-brand-positioning)) (weak source)
- Amazon evolved its positioning with Prime from transactional convenience to lifestyle integration, making the service indispensable to daily life. ([source](https://titannetwork.com/amazon-positioning)) (weak source)

### News
- In July 2025, Amazon cut its Google Shopping ad spend to near zero, disappearing from Google Shopping results without explanation. ([source](https://priceshape.com/resources/blog/amazons-pricing-strategy-smaller-competitors)) (weak source)
- Amazon launched 'Alexa+', a next-generation AI-powered personal assistant, with new Echo devices designed specifically for it. ([source](https://press.aboutamazon.com/press-release-archive?tag=bhg-onsite-prod-20&ascsubtag=1&p=2))
- Amazon introduced same-day grocery delivery for Prime customers in Catania and a $25 complete Thanksgiving meal with same-day delivery. ([source](https://press.aboutamazon.com/press-release-archive?tag=bhg-onsite-prod-20&ascsubtag=1&p=2))

## Meta Platforms
Website: https://www.meta.com
Reason: Meta is Google's closest competitor in digital advertising, with both companies dominating global online ad spending, and also competes in AI development.

### Pricing
- Meta One business and creator bundles are priced at $14.99/month for Essential, $49.99/month for Advanced, $149/month for Expert, and $499/month for Max. ([source](https://mashable.com/tech/meta-one-pricing-features-creators-small-businesses)) (weak source)
- Meta is testing AI subscription tiers at $7.99/month and $19.99/month that offer expanded access to compute-intensive AI features like advanced reasoning and image/video generation. ([source](https://www.foxbusiness.com/fox-news-tech/meta-paid-subscriptions-facebook-instagram-whatsapp))

### Features
- Meta One subscriptions provide enhanced features such as more AI usage, expressive tools in Stories, voice effects, increased image and video generation, and deeper app customization. ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai))
- Meta One business plans include verified badges, impersonation protection, AI-powered customer conversation scaling, and advanced analytics tools across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Meta AI. ([source](https://business.meta.com/news/introducing-meta-one-plans-for-businesses))
- Meta AI subscriptions offer users greater capacity for complex requests, more room to create, and advanced AI features like comprehensive responses and expanded content generation. ([source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/27/meta-testing-ai-subscription-services-cheapest-plan-at-7point99-a-month.html))

### Positioning
- Meta positions Meta One as a way for creators and businesses to stand out, create more, and grow using enhanced tools and expanded AI access across its family of apps. ([source](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai))
- Meta emphasizes that its core apps and basic Meta AI experience remain free, with subscriptions offering premium features without ad removal. ([source](https://mashable.com/tech/meta-one-pricing-features-creators-small-businesses)) (weak source)
- Meta competes with Google Gemini, OpenAI, and Anthropic by offering AI subscription services that enhance user creativity and productivity through expanded AI capabilities. ([source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/27/meta-testing-ai-subscription-services-cheapest-plan-at-7point99-a-month.html))

### News
- Meta One subscription plans are now rolling out globally, offering tiered access to AI tools, app features, and business growth resources across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Meta AI. ([source](https://business.meta.com/news/introducing-meta-one-plans-for-businesses))
