# Competitor briefing: Microsoft

Date: 2026-11-23

## Apple
Website: https://www.apple.com
Reason: Apple competes directly with Microsoft in consumer electronics, software ecosystems, and services, offering integrated hardware and software solutions that rival Microsoft's offerings.

### Pricing
- The Apple One Premier plan costs $37.95 per month and includes Apple Music Family, Apple TV, Apple Arcade, 2TB iCloud storage, Apple News+, and Apple Fitness+. ([source](https://appleinsider.com/inside/apple-one))
- iCloud+ 50GB costs $0.99/month, 200GB costs $2.99/month, 2TB costs $9.99/month, 6TB costs $29.99/month, and 12TB costs $59.99/month. ([source](https://www.apple.com/icloud))

### Features
- The new iPad mini has a stunning OLED display, A20 Pro chip, water and dust resistance, and faster wireless connectivity. ([source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-gives-ipad-mini-its-biggest-update-ever))

### Positioning
- Apple positions itself as the brand for creative people who want simple, beautiful tools, not the most features. ([source](https://www.brandbosshq.com/insights/brand-positioning-apple))
- Apple is positioned around integrated hardware, software, and services; product design; performance; privacy; ease of use; and a premium end-to-end customer experience. ([source](https://edrawmind.wondershare.com/strategy-planning/apple-segmentation-targeting-and-positioning.html))
- Apple positions the iPhone at the high end of the market, combining aspirational appeal with design, technology, and simplicity. ([source](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/apple-business-model))

### News
- Apple unveiled the new iPad mini with A20 Pro chip and OLED display on October 26, 2026, available for pre-order starting October 28. ([source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-gives-ipad-mini-its-biggest-update-ever))
- Apple announced new MacBook Pro models with PROLED XDR Display on October 28, 2026, with availability starting November 6. ([source](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display))

## Google
Website: https://www.google.com
Reason: Google challenges Microsoft through its Google Workspace productivity suite and cloud services, providing cloud-based alternatives to Microsoft 365 and Azure.

### Pricing
- Google AI Pro is priced at $19.99 per month, includes 5 TB storage, and bundles Gemini in Gmail, Docs, and Sheets. ([source](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans))

### Features
- Gemini in Chrome is being expanded to users in the U.K., enabling AI-powered assistance during browsing. ([source](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products))
- Google Vids now includes Gemini Omni, allowing anyone to create HD videos easily. ([source](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products))
- The Gemini API now supports Tool Retrieval, which loads agent tool schemas on demand to reduce latency and token usage. ([source](https://iacompa.com/news/companies/google))

### Positioning
- Google positions itself as building AI agents that perform tasks autonomously rather than just assisting users. ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gwXQzcA3pMY))
- Google Cloud emphasizes AI and agents, multicloud support, and global infrastructure as key differentiators. ([source](https://cloud.google.com/products))
- Google AI plans bundle storage, AI capabilities, and services like YouTube Premium Lite to offer integrated value. ([source](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans))

### News
- Google launched Tool Retrieval in the Gemini API on November 19, 2026, currently in preview. ([source](https://iacompa.com/news/companies/google))
- At Google I/O 2026, the company unveiled major upgrades to Gemini, Search, Android, and AI development tools. ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gwXQzcA3pMY))

## Amazon.com
Website: https://www.amazon.com
Reason: Amazon competes with Microsoft in cloud computing through Amazon Web Services (AWS), a leading platform that rivals Microsoft Azure in enterprise infrastructure.

### Pricing
- Amazon Prime costs $14.99 per month or $139 per year in the United States. ([source](https://ecommerceparadise.com/amazonprime-pricing))
- Prime for Young Adults (18 to 24 or college students) costs $7.49 per month or $69 per year. ([source](https://ecommerceparadise.com/amazonprime-pricing))
- Prime Access is available for $6.99 per month for individuals on qualifying government assistance programs. ([source](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G34EUPKVMYFW8N2U))

### Features
- Amazon Prime includes free same-day, one-day, and two-day delivery on millions of items. ([source](https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime))
- Prime Video offers new releases, award-winning Originals, and live sports. ([source](https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime))
- The Amazon Selling Partner plugin allows AI assistants to access Seller Central data for managing inventory and operations. ([source](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/announcements/amazons-selling-partner-plugin-ads-mcp))

### Positioning
- Amazon positions itself as offering everyday low prices, free delivery, and exclusive deals to help customers save over $1,000 annually. ([source](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-q3-2026-earnings-preview))
- Amazon emphasizes convenience through integrated shopping experiences, such as carrying over text-based requests to Alexa for Shopping. ([source](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop))
- Amazon Prime is positioned to offer a bundle of benefits including delivery, entertainment, and exclusive savings events like Prime Day. ([source](https://www.amazon.com/amazonprime))

### News
- Amazon expanded its ultra-fast delivery service, Amazon Now, to over 450 towns and cities globally. ([source](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-q3-2026-earnings-preview))
- Amazon increased product selection from notable brands by more than 20% year-over-year. ([source](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-q3-2026-earnings-preview))
- Amazon launched a new feature allowing users to text Alexa for Shopping to manage lists, registries, and purchases. ([source](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop))
