# Competitor briefing: Meta

Date: 2026-11-23

## Alphabet (Google and YouTube)
Website: https://www.alphabet.com
Reason: Alphabet is a major competitor to Meta in digital advertising and online content, with YouTube and Google Search directly competing for ad revenue and user attention.

### Pricing
- Alphabet introduced upgraded Gemini 3.5 and 'Omni' AI models with competitive pricing against OpenAI and Anthropic to attract developers and enterprises. ([source](https://www.investors.com/news/technology/google-stock-google-io-ai-model-gemini-pricing))

### Features
- YouTube advertising revenue growth is supported by AI-driven ad performance improvements across Google’s platforms. ([source](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/META/pressreleases/472887/meta-platforms-vs-alphabet-which-digital-ad-behemoth-has-an-edge))
- Google upgraded AI Overviews to Gemini 3, offering a seamless transition to AI Mode with conversational capabilities at the top of search results. ([source](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/META/pressreleases/472887/meta-platforms-vs-alphabet-which-digital-ad-behemoth-has-an-edge))

### Positioning
- Alphabet positions itself as a diversified competitor to Meta, with revenue streams from search, YouTube, cloud, and subscriptions beyond advertising. ([source](https://tickeron.com/blogs/alphabet-goog-vs-meta-meta-weighing-diversification-against-ad-momentum-20397))
- Alphabet emphasizes its AI and full-stack approach to drive momentum across search, YouTube, and cloud, differentiating from Meta’s ad-centric model. ([source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000080/googexhibit991q32026.htm))
- Alphabet competes with Meta by leveraging Android and search dominance to influence user access and ad targeting capabilities. ([source](https://www.hudson-labs.com/research/alphabets-top-competitors-peers))

### News
- In Q3 2026, YouTube ad revenue reached $11.2 billion, growing 9% year-over-year but falling short of estimates. ([source](https://www.facebook.com/Techmeme/posts/alphabet-reports-q3-youtube-ad-revenue-up-9-yoy-to-112b-below-estimates-and-goog/1571707998324842))
- Alphabet reported Google advertising revenue of $82.012 billion in Q3 2026, up from $74.18 billion in the same period of 2025. ([source](https://www.contentgrip.com/alphabet-ad-revenue-youtube))
- Meta is reportedly in talks to spend billions on Google's AI chips (TPUs) as an alternative to NVIDIA GPUs, signaling a potential partnership in AI infrastructure. ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OqI2H-PSPfM))

## ByteDance (TikTok)
Website: https://www.bytedance.com
Reason: ByteDance, through TikTok, competes directly with Meta in short-form video content and user engagement, particularly among younger audiences.

### Pricing
- TikTok offers lower advertising costs compared to Instagram, making it valuable for budget-constrained advertisers. ([source](https://www.promarket.org/2025/04/02/a-tiktok-ban-would-raise-ad-prices-on-meta-and-harm-small-business))
- ByteDance benefits from a 63% reduction in per-token AI inference costs with Doubao version 1.6, enabling lower pricing for AI services. ([source](https://research.contrary.com/company/bytedance))
- TikTok's average U.S. CPM is lower than Meta's, contributing to its appeal among cost-sensitive advertisers. ([source](https://www.promarket.org/2025/04/02/a-tiktok-ban-would-raise-ad-prices-on-meta-and-harm-small-business))

### Features
- TikTok uses a highly effective recommendation algorithm that drives user engagement, particularly with short-form video content. ([source](https://tsginvest.com/bytedance))
- TikTok integrates e-commerce through TikTok Shop, enabling shoppertainment and direct product sales within the app. ([source](https://www.ocbridge.ai/bytedance-vs-meta-the-digital-arena-revenue-race))
- TikTok offers Spark Ads, In-Feed Ads, and TikTok Shop ads to support brand engagement and conversion. ([source](https://admanage.ai/blog/facebook-advertising-competitors))

### Positioning
- TikTok positions itself as a shoppertainment platform that combines entertainment with e-commerce, challenging Meta's social media and advertising dominance. ([source](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4485772-meta-platforms-bytedance-impact-tiktok-instagram))
- TikTok is particularly positioned as a cost-effective and high-engagement advertising platform for small and budget-constrained advertisers. ([source](https://www.promarket.org/2025/04/02/a-tiktok-ban-would-raise-ad-prices-on-meta-and-harm-small-business))
- ByteDance competes directly with Meta in short-form video content and user engagement, especially among younger audiences. ([source](https://research.contrary.com/company/bytedance))

### News
- ByteDance will retain a ~20% stake in US TikTok and receive algorithm licensing fees under the Oracle-Walmart deal, without operational control. ([source](https://research.contrary.com/company/bytedance))
- Trump approved a TikTok takeover by Oracle and Walmart, resulting in ByteDance losing operational control but retaining financial interests. ([source](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4485772-meta-platforms-bytedance-impact-tiktok-instagram))

## Snap Inc.
Website: https://www.snap.com
Reason: Snap Inc. operates Snapchat, a social media and augmented reality platform that explicitly competes with Meta’s core apps like Instagram and Facebook.

### Pricing
- Snap's AR Specs glasses are priced at $2,195 per unit. ([source](https://www.facebook.com/yahoofinance/videos/snapchat-ceo-evan-spiegel-showcased-the-new-2195-specs-ar-glasses-/1369914378330861))

### Features
- Snapchat+ offers exclusive, experimental, and pre-release features to subscribers. ([source](https://www.thirdbridge.com/en-us/companies/snap-inc.a998508f-8617-49e0-a348-46f0bc9b54c3))
- Lens+ provides access to AI-powered video effects and exclusive features within Snapchat+. ([source](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-platforms-vs-snap-social-170800671.html))
- Snapchat+ plans include adding live-streaming functionality and open-prompt image generation. ([source](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/408856/snap-ceo-lays-out-plans-to-overcome-market-challen.html?edition=139842))

### Positioning
- Snap positions itself as a messaging company focused on close-friend communication and youth relevance. ([source](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/02/06/meta-is-trouncing-snap-in-digital-ads-heres-why.html))
- Snap differentiates itself through a camera-first interface, AR depth, and youth positioning against Meta. ([source](https://dcfanalyst.com/blogs/overview/snap-overview))
- Snap targets users with AR behavior and a product graph built around private, habitual visual communication. ([source](https://dcfanalyst.com/blogs/overview/snap-overview))

### News
- Snap announced its AR Specs glasses in June 2025, positioning them as a competitor to Apple and Meta AR wearables. ([source](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/408856/snap-ceo-lays-out-plans-to-overcome-market-challen.html?edition=139842))
- Snap's stock dropped nearly 11% after the $2,195 AR glasses were unveiled in June 2026. ([source](https://www.facebook.com/yahoofinance/videos/snapchat-ceo-evan-spiegel-showcased-the-new-2195-specs-ar-glasses-/1369914378330861))
- Meta and Snap are set to go to trial in October 2026 over claims that their platforms contributed to youth social media addiction. ([source](https://m1.com/invest/stocks/SNAP))
